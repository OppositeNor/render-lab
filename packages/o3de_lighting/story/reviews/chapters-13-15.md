Swap: `{@ probeParallax=1}` at "Without that correction", `{@ probeParallax=0 blend=1.5}` before "walls slide out of place" (hold, then return to 1 at the end). The last still (parallax 3rd frame) shows a near-identical room, so the beat's point is currently invisible. Also move the sweep from the last sentence to right after the third sentence, and use a close, low-pitch camera on the floor mirror with `probeBoxes=1` so the outer box and the shifted wall edge are both visible. Sentences 1-3 (cubemap from one point; ray traced to the outer box; fit the room) are only a pseudo-code highlight: add one cue that draws the ray and its hit on the box (or the existing probe-box overlay) while "traced to the outer box" is spoken.

Cut "O3DE draws one face per frame... five frames later" to one clause, merge the DDS sentence and the preset sentence ("The editor writes the six faces to a DDS file, and O3DE's image builder makes a 256-texel, six-mip GGX-filtered cubemap, the skylight's own preset"). 3. **c13-ball (25 s) is a tangent for lighting.** Keep two sentences (sphere shows the sharpest mip; the capture leaves it out) and drop the intro and "white metal with roughness zero" sentence, or fold the mip sweep (`probeBallMip` 2, 4, 0) into c13-bake right after "six mips, each filtered with GGX". 4. **c13-stencil: the number three comes from nowhere.** "Surfaces outside every inner box keep the value three" and the later "one minus summed weight" depend on a stencil base value never explained. Add to the first sentence of the beat: "Every surface that takes part starts at two" (verify against `probe-stencil`, `probe-stencil-reduced`) or reword: "Surfaces inside no inner box end at three, the value the later passes test for." The stencil view (viewMode=13) shows cyan floor and shapes with no legend; add a callout label ("inner box = 4, outside = 3") or highlight the `px.stencil` lines in the listing while the sentence is read (the listing highlight `stencil` covers lines already; confirm it covers the `s == 3` test).

Add `probeBoxes=1` and a values-panel highlight (readout "composited") on "surfaces inside an inner box get that grey added", sweep `ibl` 1 -> 0 over the sentence (it is a toggle: add `blend=1.5`), and say "so a surface inside an inner box looks washed out" to name the effect. 6. **c13-blend: two sentences point at nothing.** "Probes are drawn largest first, so a smaller probe has the last word" and "the probes apply no specular occlusion" have only a callout. Add a highlight of the `# smaller ones overwrite` line for the first, and for the second say what specular occlusion is in one clause (it is "unlike the global pass" and the viewer may not recall the sky chapter) or point to the `# no specular occlusion` comment line in the probe-specular listing. ## Chapter 14 · Resolve, ambient occlusion, and the merge

### Fixes, most important first
1. **c14-resolve (new beat): the animation shows nothing.** The beat is a plain scene without panel (`panel=hidden`), `specularResolve=0 -> 1` gives no visible change (stills 1 and 2 are identical), and the difference view (viewMode=16, still 3) is a dark-blue frame with a few dots, because the true difference is under a few percent at this camera and 4 samples. A viewer cannot see "rims and smallest highlights". Fix:
   - Zoom: camera on the sphere's highlight and rim (`camera.target` at the sphere, `camera.distance` ~1.2) so single pixels are large; use the `sphereRoughness=0.06` highlight and a thin bright rim.
   - Show the before/after as a flip on the highlight: `specularResolve=0` for sentence 1-2, `1` at "Then the samples are averaged in the log domain", and cut to viewMode=16 only when it shows a visible heat ramp; if the difference is still under 2 % at default, raise the difference-view gain in the shader (`relative * 10.0` -> 20) or add a legend ("0 to 10 %") on screen; a colour ramp legend is needed while "from nought to ten percent" is spoken.
   - Add a pseudo-code listing for the resolve (`pseudocode/` has none: write `msaa_resolve.py` with the diagonal max, the clamp `min(1, 1.5*brightest/lum)`, and `exp(mean(log(c)))`) and highlight each line as its sentence is read; currently the key concepts (diagonal neighbours, factor 1.5, log mean) have only a callout. A formula panel `param.formula` would be better for "geometric mean".
   - A tiny diagram would make sentences 3-4 intuitive: a pixel with four samples, one very bright (a 10x highlight sample), the clamp lowering it to 1.5x the neighbours' brightest, the arithmetic mean vs the log mean as two bars. Without it "so one bright sample cannot dominate an edge" asserts the point; give one number ("a sample ten times brighter than its neighbours would pull a plain average to a third; the clamp and log mean hold it near one and a half").
2. **c14-resolve flow.** The first sentence ("every term sits in two targets, at several samples a pixel") introduces MSAA and the targets in one line; add what the targets are ("a diffuse target and a specular target") and why a plain average is wrong for specular (HDR highlights alias on edges; averaging in linear HDR lets a bright sample dominate). The last sentence ("four samples where O3DE renders two") is a fidelity note; keep, but it is the only on-screen statement of the lab's difference and lacks a label. Also "scaled down to at most one and a half times the brightest luminance among the samples of its four diagonal neighbours" is dense: say "The diagonal neighbours are the four pixels touching its corners" and, separately, "a sample brighter than that is dimmed, never brightened".
3. **c14-compute: the radius sweep is backwards.** `ssaoRadius=0.12` is set at "A wider radius reaches" and `0.03` at "farther and darkens more broadly", so the narrower radius is on screen while "farther... more broadly" is spoken. Swap: `ssaoRadius=0.03` at "A wider radius", `0.12 blend=1.5` right before "reaches farther". The AO result frames (white room with soft contact shadows) are good for this sweep, but the stills show the values panel is not on screen in the highlighted frames: confirm `panel=values` has the radius row.
4. **c14-blur: the blur sweep is backwards.** `ssaoBlur` is 1 = blur on (`demos.rs:39`). The beat sets `ssaoBlur=0` at "Without the blur, the" and `1` at "samples' pattern shows through", so the pattern appears when the blur comes on. Swap: `ssaoBlur=1` first (the default), `ssaoBlur=0 blend=1` right before "samples' pattern shows through". The AO is white-dominant in this view; use a closer camera on a wall-floor crease so the noise pattern is visible, with `viewMode=15`.
5. **c14-compute is the most dense beat (50 s) with no picture of the samples.** "Twelve close by, and ten rotations of four shared with its neighbours" and "weighted by one over its squared distance plus 0.07" are heard but the screen shows the final AO.
