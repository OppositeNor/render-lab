Overall comments:
- The chapter is accurate against the research (formulas, 52 samples, 27 px, R8, 2.2 power) but mostly asserts rather than shows. The core demonstrations (AO on/off at the crease, the weighted accumulation, the blur/upsample edge preservation, sunlit crease darkening) are invisible in the stills because the subject is tiny, the insets are white, or no before/after exists.
- The key concept (AO as a sky-visibility fraction approximated by a cosine-weighted average of depth-rebuilt samples) is never defined. Define it early and light the formula terms as they are spoken.
- Fix the example pixel (a real crease with AO about 0.3-0.6), `ssaoSamples` semantics, and the insets (one at a time, contrast-stretched, zoomed) first. These give the biggest gain for the least effort.
- Trade-offs (direct light darkened, off-screen blindness, screen-fraction radius) are stated but not shown. Each needs a one-shot demonstration.
- Clean up the citations (`ssao-defaults` chip, `sources:` header), and drop the clutter (labels, vectors) in the material beat.
