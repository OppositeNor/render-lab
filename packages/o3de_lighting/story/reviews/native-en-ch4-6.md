# Native English review, chapters 4 to 6

13 suggestions. Sentences not listed read naturally.

1. c4-g 1, "G removes the facets that": the relative clause with "their neighbours hide" is clumsy to hear. Suggestion: G discards the facets that neighbouring facets hide from the light or from the eye.
2. c4-f 1, "F, the Fresnel term, says": "from the angle" trails after a long clause and attaches ambiguously. Suggestion: F, the Fresnel term, says how much of the light the surface reflects, and it depends on the angle between H and the eye.
3. c4-f 2, "In Schlick's form, it is": "they" has no clear referent and "it is F zero" is loose. Suggestion: In Schlick's form, it equals F zero when H and the eye line up, and rises toward white as they move apart.
4. c4-f 3, "As the light sinks toward": "toward the side" is not how anyone says it. Suggestion: As the light sinks toward the horizon, L moves away from V, so H turns away from V too, and F climbs.
5. c4-models 3, "Skin uses a different diffuse,": "diffuse" used as a bare noun sounds clipped. Suggestion: Skin uses a different diffuse term, Disney's normalised diffuse, and so does Enhanced when subsurface scattering is on.
6. c4-lut 2, "Across the texture runs N": the inverted order is literary and trips when spoken. Suggestion: N dot V runs across the texture, and one minus roughness runs down it.
7. c4-multi 5, "Take one divided by": "divided by" is wordy for speech. Suggestion: Take one over the kept share, which is A plus B, and subtract one.
8. c4-multi 7, "To get the factor, that": the passive pair is stiff after the imperative in the line before. Suggestion: To get the factor, multiply that loss by F zero and add one.
9. c4-recap 1, "So each light adds diffuse": "weighed" is the wrong word here. Suggestion: So each light adds diffuse and specular light, weighted by D, G, F, and the diffuse response, all from the surface's numbers.
10. c5-why 14, "As for how the map": "As for" is a stiff topic marker. Suggestion: The map is made by drawing every shadow-casting mesh into it with only a depth test and a small depth bias.
11. c5-cascades 5, "Each cut point between cascades": "by a ratio" dangles at the end. Suggestion: Each cut point between cascades blends uniform and logarithmic spacing according to a ratio.
12. c5-lookup 3, "The offset is the normal": the stacked commas make "times two, over the map's width" hard to follow by ear. Suggestion: The offset is the normal bias setting times two, divided by the map's width in texels.
13. c6-two 2, "The specular cubemap has six": "a side at the top" is clipped. Suggestion: The specular cubemap has six mips, and by default the top one is two hundred and fifty-six texels on each side.

## Habits seen across the chapters

- Written-style inversions and passives where speech would use an imperative or an active verb (c4-lut 2, c4-multi 7).
- "They", "it", or "that" with an unclear referent after a long clause, especially in the Fresnel and cascade passages (c4-f 2).
- Stiff connectives ("As for", "by a ratio") and clipped noun uses ("a different diffuse", "a side at the top").
- Occasional wrong near-word ("weighed" for "weighted", "toward the side" for "toward the horizon").
- Otherwise the chapters keep short, active sentences, which is why only a few lines needed changes.
