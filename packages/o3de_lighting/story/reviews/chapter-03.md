Overall comments: The chapter has the right content and a sound order, from the metallic workflow to roughness to AA and then decals. But several claims rest on invisible data: F0 at 8 bits, the 0.024 clamp, the 0.18 cap, the ACEScg conversion and the normal. Those are the first things to fix.
The code and values panels are not used where they matter (roughness has no readout, AA has no formula, occlusion has no formula), and the highlight does not step with each sentence.
The chapter's most original claim, that decals are missing from the targets, needs a before-and-after comparison and a visible ordering, not a single inset.
Two items teach the wrong model by omission: specular AA is presented as a fix for a point-light highlight on a mirror sphere, and occlusion is presented as if it darkened all light.
Fit looks near the 220 s budget (about 580 words), but the occlusion and emission content is rushed and should be split.
