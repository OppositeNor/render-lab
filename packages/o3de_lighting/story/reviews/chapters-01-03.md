## Chapter 1 · O3DE's opaque frame

Most important first.

1. **c1-frame (mermaid): the focus lighting is not visible, and the diagram does not match what is said.** In the still (caption "with the roughness, and the normal, which later passes read back") all eight nodes look equally lit, so I cannot confirm that `focus: CULL, FWD, TARGETS, ...` lights each node as it is named. Check that a focus step per sentence exists (one node group per sentence). The diagram also has nodes "DiffuseGlobal" and "Reflection Composite", but the last sentence says the DiffuseProbeGrid gem swaps the diffuse pass for "a composite of its own". Nothing on screen marks that swap. Fix: on that sentence, relabel or flash the DiffuseGlobal node to "DiffuseComposite (DiffuseProbeGrid gem)" or add a dashed alternate node. As it stands, that sentence points at nothing.
2. **c1-frame sentence 4 is a 40-word list** ("Full-screen passes then add the sky's diffuse light and the reflections, from the sky and from reflection probes; SSAO darkens the diffuse, and a merge pass adds the two together"). It names five nodes in one breath. Split it in two at the semicolon (sky diffuse + reflections / SSAO + merge) and give each half its own focus step, so the viewer sees DIFF, REFL, COMP then AO, MERGE light up.
3. **Depth: three things the outline promised are only named, not explained.** MSAA (callout only in the chapter's `sources:`, never said), the stencil routing, and why the targets are separate. A viewer who knows graphics asks "why separate diffuse and specular targets?" The beat answers only that the merge pass sums them. Add one clause to c1-code's last sentence or to c1-frame's "forward targets" sentence: "kept apart so that later passes can darken the diffuse with ambient occlusion before the two are added." (This is also the chapter's strongest bridge to ch14.)
4. **c1-room: two sentences point at nothing.** "Every formula you will see comes from O3DE version two point eight" has callout=engine-version, but the still shows the `ForwardPassSimplePointLight` callout from the previous sentence and the version only appears in the header strip. "With no shape and no shadows" is shown only in the panel footnote, which is small. Fix: put `{@ panel=values}` ahead of "no shadows" so the footnote reads, or drop the version sentence from c1-room (the header already carries it; it is repeated in c1-sources).
5. **c1-code: callout/label clutter.** In the code stills the sphere and teapot labels pile on top of each other and the bottom callout ("ForwardPassEvaluateLighting.azsli · EvaluateLighting_ForwardPass") is cut off at the panel edge ("...ForwardPassEvaluateLighting.azsli · EvaluateLighting_ForwardP" and "ForwardPassDirectLighting.azsli · ApplyDirectLighting_Forward"). Set `labels=0` in the c1-code beat (the object labels are not discussed there) and shorten callout text or let it wrap.
6. **c1-code: the highlight for sentence 1 lights `surface` only, but the sentence is "In pseudo-code, the forward pixel shader is short".** Highlight the whole `pixel_shader` function there (or no highlight), then step surface, init, lights, ibl, targets as now. As is, the first sentence reads as being about the surface line only.
7. **c1-views: the sweep carries the point, but the specular still is almost black.** The "specular only" view (viewMode=2) is dark except for the floor spot and `diffuseLighting` is 0.00 in the readout at that moment, so the viewer cannot see how little specular there is versus the diffuse. Keep as is, but add a sentence that contrasts them: "Together they are the pixel's colour: the diffuse carries the surface, the specular adds the glints." Also the diffuse still (viewMode=1) is darker than the "everything" view: say once that the sky adds the rest, which the last sentence does.
8. **Flow.** The first sentence ("This is the Open 3D Engine's Atom renderer ...") is a fine opener. c1-frame to c1-code bridges well ("In pseudo-code ..."). The chapter ends on the sources, with "Every claim in this video cites a file..." which is a trust statement, not a bridge to chapter 2. Add as the last sentence of c1-views or c1-sources a hand-off: "The first step in the forward pass's list is the light list itself, so that is where we start." (Chapter 2's opener then does not have to carry the bridge alone.)

## Chapter 2 · Light culling

1. **Formulas are set while the code panel hides them.** `param.formula=1`, `=2` in c2-cull and `=3` in c2-forward and c2-prepare are given on the same sentence as `panel=code`, or while the code panel is up, so no formula term is lit (the stills of c2-cull show only the listing). Either move the `panel=values` cue to those sentences (sphere-box test, index packing and bin formulas are exactly the ones in the panel), or drop the `param.formula` cues. Best: in c2-cull, put `panel=values param.formula=1` on "A light is kept when its sphere touches the tile's box" and `param.formula=2` on the packing sentence, then go back to the listing for the group order sentence.
2. **Bin count is described but never changes.** The outline had a beat "c2-bins" (camera pulls back so the tile's span grows and the bin count steps 1 to 8); the storyboard merged it into c2-prepare as one sentence, and every readout in the stills reads "depth bins 1". Fix: after the "one, two, four, or eight depth bins" sentence, add a sweep: inspect a point near a wall edge or pull the camera back, e.g. `camera.distance` 4 to 9 with `panel=values param.formula=3`, until the readout steps 1, 2, 4. Add a sentence: "Pull the camera back and the tile's depth span grows, so the bins step up." Without it, the log2 formula has no payoff.
3. **c2-prepare sentence 5 ("The box drawn here is the inspected point's tile in view space, with its bins as slices") points at nothing I can find.** In the stills the false-colour map shows rings and dashed lines to lights but no readable box or slices. Either draw it (cue a camera view that shows the tile box, e.g. an oblique one) or delete the sentence and replace it with the readout row ("the 32-slice mask 0xFFFFFFFF"), which is visible.
4. **c2-cull: the group order is told in two sentences with no visual.** "Lights are grouped by type, decals first, then simple point lights, spot lights, and the area lights, each group closed by an end marker" is the key to both findings in c2-finding, yet the listing's `cull` function only shows `tile.list.append(END_OF_GROUP)` and one type. Add a small strip in the values panel or a new highlight in the listing: a row `decals | point | spot | disk | capsule | quad | polygon` with END_OF_GROUP between, lit one slot at a time as each is named. c2-finding then lights "polygon" (empty) and the skipped group on the same strip.
5. **c2-cull: the "threads append in whatever order" sentence is a tangent in the middle of the algorithm.** It interrupts packing, groups, then the radius sweep. Move it to the end of c2-forward or c2-finding (where the determinism of the lab is relevant), or cut to: "O3DE's order within a group is not fixed, so the lab uses index order."
6. **c2-cull: the radius sweep is only 5 s at the end and follows an unrelated sentence.** The still sheet does not catch it. It is the one place the "kept when it touches" sentence is shown changing; place it right after the sphere-box sentence (before packing), sweep `extraLightRadius` 0.3 to 2.4 over 6 s with `panel=values`, and say "As the lights' radius grows, more tiles keep them" over the sweep, with the heat map visibly turning yellow.
7. **c2-finding: two oddities in 30 s, with little to see.** "One finding: the forward pass also walks a group for polygon lights" comes with the values panel, and the second finding's last sentence ("Switch that option off on a material, and the area lights after them read their neighbours' groups; decals, by contrast, keep walking theirs") is the hardest sentence in the chapter: a viewer cannot follow it without the group strip of fix 4. Rewrite as: "Turn punctual lights off on a material, and the forward pass skips the two simple-light groups without skipping their end markers, so each area-light type reads the group before it." (Check the exact wording against `punctual-group-skip` first.) Also state why the findings matter ("so polygon lights in a default project never shade") in plain words; the first one does.
8. **Flow and fit.** The opener ("That was the whole frame; the next part looks at its first lighting step") bridges well. c2-why's formula panel shows all five formulas at once before any is introduced, which is clutter; start with `panel=values` hidden formulas or only the readout. At 1.10x of budget the chapter is tight; making room for fixes 2 and 6 means cutting fix 5's sentence and the "lab compares every tile's list on the GPU with a CPU copy" sentence (the second half "and checks that no light reaching a surface is missing from its bin" is the claim worth keeping, shorten it to that).

## Chapter 3 · The surface

1. **c3-aa: the toggle does not show the effect, and the kernel has no formula.** `specularAA=0` then `=1` happen inside one sentence over a few seconds on a roughness-0.1 sphere, and in the still the highlight difference at the rim is not noticeable. Fix: (a) add a formula to the formula panel for it, e.g. `\alpha^2 \leftarrow \min(\alpha^2 + \min(\tfrac12(|\partial_x n|^2 + |\partial_y n|^2),\ 0.18),\ 1)`, lit when "It adds a kernel of half the squared change in the normal" is said (research R3.06 gives kernel = min(2 x 0.25 x (dx n.dx n + dy n.dy n), 0.18)); (b) make the demonstration stronger: zoom to the rim (`camera.distance` 1.6), hold AA off 2 s, on 2 s, off 2 s, with a `viewMode` or readout row for `roughnessA2` that visibly changes at the rim; (c) the bumpy case (a normal-mapped or faceted object) shows the effect better than a smooth sphere, if the lab has one.
2. **c3-rough: the lobe is shown but never named.** The beat turns on `lobe=1` and the still shows a pink ellipse labelled "specular x N.V", and no sentence mentions it. Add after "Roughness is authored on a perceptual scale": "The pink shape is the highlight's lobe; watch it widen." The final sweep (0.05 to 0.85) then lands on a thing the viewer has been told to look at.
3. **c3-rough: the two squarings are the claim, and they are not shown changing.** Every number (roughnessA 0.1254, roughnessA2 0.0157) is in the readout but unlit. While "squares it into alpha, and squares it again" is said, light `roughnessA` then `roughnessA2` rows (`readoutRows` or a row highlight cue), and hold roughness at 0.5 for it (the next sentence). Also the "why": add "so that equal steps of the slider give equal-looking steps of highlight." Also the clamp (0.024) is not shown: sweep roughness down to 0.0 for 2 s first (readout stays at 0.0240 / alpha 0.0006), then up to 0.85.
4. **c3-rough: sentence split by cues.** "O3DE clamps it to at least zero point zero two [orbit] four, squares it..." has an animation cue in the middle of the number, which cuts a pause into "zero point zero two ... four". Move the second orbit cue to the start of "squares it into alpha" (it does not need to fall inside the number).
5. **c3-metal: depth is good (the sphere sweeps 0 to 1 while the albedo row goes to 0.000) but "so a metal has no diffuse colour at all" is not shown on the sphere**: the rendered sphere at metallic 1 turns into a mirror of the room, which hides the lack of diffuse. Add a short `viewMode=1` (diffuse only) at metallic 1: the sphere goes black. Say "in the diffuse view it disappears". Similarly "dust on metal" in the last sentence is an aside; cut or keep to 3 words.
6. **c3-occlusion: the beat is the longest (about 42 s, 8 sentences) and the first five sentences are mostly listing, with a 36-second light path.** The visible result (grid cavity map on the sphere) is only shown in the second half. The final sweep (`diffuseOcclusionFactor` 2 then 0.2) is good: it is the only place a value visibly changes. Fix: start the sweep earlier, at "Each is the map's red channel, raised to the power of its factor", so the viewer sees factor 1 then 2 deepen the creases as the power is stated; keep the closing 0.2 fade. Also say which one the sphere shows: the grid is the diffuse map, the specular occlusion has no visible stand-in (add `specularOcclusion` to the panel or drop "sometimes called a cavity map").
7. **c3-examples: shown and said correctly, but the teapot sentence ("its green leaves the diffuse light and moves into the highlight") is the same fact as c3-metal.** Fine as a recap, but the stills show the sphere in front and the teapot hidden behind the sphere label stack, so the teapot change is hard to see. Move the camera (`camera.target` at the teapot, `labels=0`) for the teapot and box changes.
8. **Flow, fit.** The opener ("With the lights sorted into lists, the next part turns to what they light: the surface") bridges from ch2. Sentences 2 to 4 of c3-inputs are good (the readout rows 4 then 10 reveal the derived values). c3-inputs ends on a `pause=0.6` after "what the lighting actually uses"; then c3-metal starts with a code panel and no mention of which derived value is next: add "First, albedo and specular F zero." The outline's c3-maps beat (normal map, emissive, parallax "said in passing") is gone; one closing sentence in c3-examples would cover it: "The lab leaves out the normal, emissive and parallax inputs; they change the surface record, not the rules." Budget is fine (1.22x).
