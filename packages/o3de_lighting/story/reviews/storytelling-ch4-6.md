## Core formulas

- Specular product (c4-f 4): stated and glossed. The listener card says the audience knows microfacet BRDFs, so it works as a bridge. The `four N dot L N dot V` divisor (c4-g 5) is stated with no why, and N dot L enters the product with none.
- BRDF look-up texture (c4-lut 3, 4, 14): the split into F zero times A plus B is told as a result. The step that makes the reflection linear in F zero, and a worked texel, are missing (row 18).
- Multiscatter (c4-multi 5 to 7): told as a recipe, no worked case and no stress (what is lost without it). The F zero factor has no reason (row 23).
- Split sum (c6-splitsum 3): the integral and the approximation are never said (row 73).
- Cascade split (c5-cascades 5 to 10): the formula has a worked case (numbers), but the spacings are never shown (row 33).

- debug colour (c5-why 7) vs debug colouring (c5-cascades 11); shadow pass (c5-lookup 1) vs full-screen pass (c5-fullscreen 1); raw buffer (c5-blur 1).
