# Review: chapters 3 and 4 (VoxelGI)

Sources: storyboard, outline, research.md, pseudocode, and the c3/c4 stills. The stills were viewed three per beat, early to late. Stills were not opened for the 2D beats.

## Chapter 3: What the bake stores

| Beat | Issue | Severity | Why it matters | Specific correction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| c3-sort | The code panel shows the `fixup` listing with leaf lines lit, not a sort. No line of code is being explained. | high | The viewer sees "finish the leaf" highlighted while hearing "sorted by level". | Use a sort/level-run listing (`sort_cells`: key = (level, x, y, z); `level_offset = sum(counts[:L])`). Highlight the key, then the offset line, one per sentence. |
| c3-sort | In all three stills the camera sits inside giant black cells, and the level-bar legend covers the left third. The cell array is never seen as an array. `cutHeight` sweeps nothing meaningful for "each level is one run". | high | The scene is occluded, and the sentence points at nothing visible. | Stop `cameraDrift` and fix the camera at about distance 5. Draw the array as a horizontal strip with level colours, and sweep a "run" bracket over level 7, then 4. Move the legend to the strip. |
| c3-sort | "Sorted" is stated, but the reason (one dispatch per level, children before parents) is not. | medium | The viewer will not see why order matters to the GPU. | Replace the memory-stretch clause with "so the GPU can light all level-7 cells in one dispatch, then fold them into level 6". |
| c3-fixup | The cell being narrated (35668) is not marked in any still. Normals are never drawn, so the "normals cancel" sentence has nothing to show, and `cutHeight`/`cellView` sweeps do not show it. | high | The 3rd sentence is the least visible, and it teaches only a number (0.01). | Add a callout ring on the leaf. Draw its normal as an arrow, then draw 3-4 sample normals that cancel and shrink the average to a dot. Sweep an "agreement" parameter 1 to 0. |
| c3-fixup | "Alpha the total of their shares" is undefined. The still shows alpha sum 10.94, which looks wrong for a coverage. | medium | The viewer cannot tell why dividing by alpha gives an average. | Say "each sample that lands in the cell adds one sixteenth of weight; the sums are weighted, so we divide by the weight". Show the sample count in the row. Also name the normalisation (unit length), which the listing omits. |
| c3-parents | Parent colour is zero, so it is drawn black or blue, and it reads as "dark cells". The third still floods the frame with large dark discs (camera inside the sphere). "Paler" does not match what the viewer sees. | medium | Data (coverage) is drawn as if it were a colour. | Map coverage to an opacity ramp with a legend ("coverage 0 to 1"). Camera outside the objects. Say "the coarser the level, the more spread-out the coverage" instead of "paler". |
| c3-parents | Why parents are colourless is not given. | high | "Set to zero" with no reason leaves a trap: the viewer concludes the coarse levels are wasted. | Add one clause: "because the colours of the coarse levels are not baked: they are sums of light, rebuilt from the leaves on every relight". Link it to ch 5 (mips). |
| c3-bytes | Four fields are shown as rows, but the scene is a static floor view with nothing changing per field. The readout also shows "light texel" and "SDF byte" rows that belong to other beats. The sweep `cellView` hits the emission sentence only. | medium | Two ideas per row (what and how many bits), and extra rows add load. | Hide light and SDF rows here. Draw a 16-byte bar split into 4 fields that fill as each is named. Decode one field live (position 63,6,29 reads back as a cell). |
| c3-bytes | "Coverage" is read as meaningful at a leaf, but a leaf's alpha was just set to one. | low | Contradicts c3-fixup. | Say "coverage, one at a leaf, a fraction at a parent". |
| c3-field | The inset covers the top of the scene. The "sphere's middle" slice is a floor plan, and no sentence ties inset cells to scene cells. Passes z, y, x look nearly identical on a sparse slice. | medium | The sweep over the three passes shows no change, so the sentence "runs along z, then y, then x" has no point. | Show a 1D column highlighted in the 3D view while each pass runs. Use a slice crossing the cube, with a coloured marker on the working column. State what an exact squared distance seed means: 1e20 = "not yet reached". |
| c3-field | Use of the field ("steer shadow rays") is promised but not explained: the viewer does not know the value means "safe jump distance". | medium | It is the key to ch 4. | Say "the byte is how many cells a ray may jump without hitting anything". |
| c3-upload | The scene shows only a 2D inset. The "two storage buffers" are never drawn. `sdf-gpu-disabled` is a trivia sentence with no visual. "Mip" is used before it is defined. The size sentence names "four megabytes... under four... one" with no matching readout (verify the numbers against the lab's 128^3/64^3 grid). The float buffer is described as "one value per cell", but it is a vec4 (16 B). | high | Weakest beat: it shows no upload, and the numbers are unverifiable on screen. | Show three boxes (octree buffer, data buffer, SDF texture), each titled with its format and size, filling with cells. Add a "light texture" box with a mip pyramid drawn. Drop the compiled-out GPU sentence, or move it to the sources card. Say "one four-float light accumulator per cell". Put the sizes in a readout. |
| c3 (whole) | Ten beats in 200 s. Data layout (sort, bytes) is dense, and the chapter never states what it is for. | medium | Purpose and result are unclear. | Add a closing line to c3-recap: "these arrays are what every later pass reads". |

## Chapter 4: Lighting the cells

| Beat | Issue | Severity | Why it matters | Specific correction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| c4-when | Sentence 2 says "moving the camera relights nothing" but no camera move or relight indicator is shown. The readout has no state row (the "0.04 s" is constant). `lightHeight` is swept in sentence 1, which is unrelated to "data or light changes". | high | The change-dependence is told, not shown. | Add a readout row "light pass: ran / skipped". Sweep `lightX` (pass runs, cells re-lit, flash), then sweep the camera only (skipped). Remove the `lightHeight` sweep from sentence 1. |
| c4-when | "Moved into the box's cells" means transformed to cell space. It is unexplained and the visual is absent. The 32-light cap is trivia here, and `light-upload` (energy x indirect energy) is never narrated though it is in the sources. | medium | Terms are used without meaning. | Say "positions converted to cell units". Move the cap to the readout only. Add an "indirect energy" sentence. |
| c4-direct | The worked floor cell is not marked in the stills. Light-vector lines are thin and tiny. The `atten` highlight (second sentence) is skipped in the sampled stills. The facing-away case is never shown. The listing's first highlight is `vector` while the sentence is "take this cell". | high | The key arithmetic is not visible. | Ring the cell. Draw the vector, attenuation as a falloff curve with the cell's distance marked, and a normal arrow with the cosine angle. Add a second cell behind the sphere or wall to show "facing away = 0". |
| c4-direct | Lighting model skips: the stored value is "light x albedo x cos". There is no 1/pi, and no one says this is outgoing radiance for the later gather. Linear-light is not stated. | medium | The viewer cannot judge units, and ch 4's "two pi" sentence is then baffling. | Add: "this is what the cell sends back out, in linear units, no division by pi". |
| c4-shadow | Direction is contradictory. The narration says the ray walks from the light toward the cell. The listing marches from `pos` along `direction`, and the outline says from the cell toward the light. | high | Wrong mental model, and it conflicts with the code panel. | Verify in `voxel_gi.glsl`, then say "from the cell toward the light". Draw the ray so that it starts at the cell. |
| c4-shadow | "Jumps that far, in whole cells along its main axis" is jargon with no animation. The jump spheres are small and the sphere fills the frame. The `partial` highlight (`occlusion=min`) is explained by a `lightZ` sweep that is invisible. | high | The core algorithm (sphere tracing on an integer field) is not understood. | Draw the circle of radius = field value at each step (the stills have faint circles). Zoom to the ray and show the step count. Add "the byte is the distance in cells, so the ray steps by it, rounded to a multiple of the step". Show a penumbra by sweeping the light's offset past a corner. |
| c4-area | The panel is outside the frame in all three stills, and no light-falling-on-cells difference is visible. "Read from the code" appears twice and points at nothing. "Division by two pi" is unexplained. LTC is not defined. | high | A negative claim ("no shadow") is shown with no contrast. | Frame the panel. Toggle the sphere as a blocker and show the cells beneath still lit, next to a punctual light's shadowed cells. Say "linearly transformed cosines, a closed form for light from a polygon". Cut the 2 pi sentence, or say "a constant factor that differs from point lights". |
| c4-all | The shot is good, but cells are tiny dots and the dark/lit contrast is faint. "64 to a GPU group" has no visual. The panel's light on the walls is not visible. | medium | The result is hard to read. | Increase cell size (octreeLevel 6 view) or add a brightness boost with a legend. Cut the 64 clause or show the thread groups sweeping. |
| c4-write | `dynamicRange` is introduced only here, with no reason. The sweep shows no clipping, because the floor cell's light is about 0.08. The 1..2 sweep is in the readout only. The alpha sentence has no visual. The anisotropy sentence is trivia with no on-screen referent. | high | Light is stored in 8 bits, so range matters to the next chapters. | Introduce it before the sweep ("8-bit texel cannot hold more than 1, so the light is divided by 2 and multiplied back on read"). Pick a bright cell near the panel, show its value against 1, and clip it when range = 1. Drop the anisotropy sentence. |
| c4 (whole) | Missing: where direct light is written (the float buffer, then the texture), and that the light passes also add emission. The hand-off line says "shadowed ... for punctual lights", and directional lights are never mentioned. | medium | The next stage's input is vague. | Add to c4-recap: "stored as float, written to the texture only after the bounce". |
