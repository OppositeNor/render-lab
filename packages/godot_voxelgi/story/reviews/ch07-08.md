# Review: chapters 7 and 8 (VoxelGI)

Overall: ch7 is accurate against research.md and the c7-low/c7-high/c7-spec sweeps are the strongest part. The weak points are (a) the code panel replaces the readout on five of eight beats, so the numbers a sentence names (edge fade, alpha, mip) vanish exactly when named; (b) several "why" links are missing (why mips, why alpha is coverage, why edge fade exists, why mix by alpha); (c) ch8 is mostly narration over a hidden panel, and its closing "follow one pixel" and "takeaways" beats show nothing of what they name.

## Top issues
1. Readouts disappear (code panel) in c7-space, c7-low, c7-scale exactly when edge fade, alpha, mip and interior are named.
2. Missing causal link in c7-low: cone footprint to mip level to cheap averaged read; alpha as coverage never defined.
3. c8-follow and c8-others point at nothing visible (hidden panel, no transparent object, no fog, no mips/leaves/lights drawn).
4. c7-scale mixes range restore, interior alpha and a GLSL quirk; the sweep does not match the sentences.
5. c8-recap takeaways are unlabelled on screen, takeaway 3 contradicts ch6 and omits leaks/memory, and "few dozen reads" is not derived.
